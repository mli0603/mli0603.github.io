# Welcome to My Blog

Hello and welcome to my blog! I'm excited to share this new space where I'll be writing about my thoughts and experiences in the world of artificial intelligence, computer vision, and research.

## What You Can Expect

In this blog, I'll be covering a variety of topics including:

- **AI Research**: Insights from my work at NVIDIA and the latest developments in spatial intelligence
- **Computer Vision**: Deep dives into 3D reconstruction, neural rendering, and visual understanding
- **Technology Trends**: My perspective on emerging technologies and their potential impact
- **Research Journey**: Behind-the-scenes looks at the research process and lessons learned

## A Bit About Me

As a Research Scientist at NVIDIA's Deep Imagination Research group, I'm passionate about enabling AI to perceive, comprehend, and interact with the physical world. My work has been integrated into products like NVIDIA Cosmos and NVIDIA Edify, and I'm always excited to share what I'm learning along the way.

## Stay Connected

I hope you'll find these posts interesting and valuable. Feel free to reach out if you have questions or want to discuss any of the topics I cover.

Thanks for reading, and welcome to the blog!
